# YWCA GitBook evidence review

## When to use
Use for source research, a claim audit, chronology, or an evidence matrix for `~/projects/ywcamissoula.com/`. Do not use a wiki synthesis as proof of a filing, ruling, or current case status.

## Procedure
1. Read the live repo page and its source links first. Orient in `~/wiki` via `AGENTS.md`, `RESOLVER.md`, `index.md`, recent `log.md`; then `ywca-missoula/index.md` and the relevant case/concept pages. `raw/source-repos/ywcamissoula-gitbook.md` is a dated snapshot, not current repo state. Search for a newer claim or conflict before relying on it.
2. Build a claim ledger: exact sentence, page/line, source artifact and pinpoint, artifact date, jurisdiction, claim type (recorded fact / firsthand account / attributed allegation / inference / unresolved), and verification status. Inspect primary records where accessible. A GitBook assertion, wiki page, and derivative article repeating one another are one source lineage, not corroboration.
3. Check exact docket, agency, filing, and disposition language against primary records. A transmittal does not establish that a court opened review or decided merits; an agency closure does not adjudicate every allegation. For legal propositions check current statute/rule and controlling authority; note jurisdiction and effective date. Never silently convert a theory into a holding.
4. For Washington custody/conditions/status claims, consult `~/wiki/ywca-missoula/cases/wa-evidence-preservation-checklist.md` (PRIVATE); do not publish or quote its private contents. Before staging an investigative case study, obtain and inspect any unpublished conversation or personal account it relies on; if the cited artifact is unavailable, explicitly mark the case-specific account unverified, avoid language implying independent investigation, and hold publication pending source and record review or remove those passages. Keep unsupported claims in attributed first-person framing and send stronger claims for human/legal review. Generalize third-party and child details when contextual clues could re-identify them.
5. Return a table of supported, qualified, contradicted, and unverified claims with exact local paths and source URLs. If evidence is inaccessible, say so and list the missing records; do not infer their contents.

## Boundaries
Do not copy `~/wiki/ywca-missoula/raw/notion/restricted/`, private legal notes, medical details, victim identifiers, or outreach material into `gitbook-export/`. Redact before public drafts. Do not send filings, contact agencies, or publish without an explicit request and review. Preserve immutable wiki `raw/` and `sources/`; new corrections belong in derived pages.

## Verification
Reopen every cited source and confirm it supports the exact sentence; verify names, dates, procedural status and links. For a research report, confirm its opening Scope, Research reviewed through date, and Disclaimer do not imply broader verification than the underlying evidence permits; do not treat the frontmatter description or a duplicated subtitle as independent corroboration. Report uncertainties and the review decision separately from factual findings.
