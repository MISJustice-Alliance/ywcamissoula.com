Review cutoff: November 5, 2026. This is an internal editorial memo in the repository root, not a GitBook page. The public article embeds the cross-institution evidence matrix; the public ODC [source ledger](../gitbook-export/montana-bar-complaints/odc-review-evidence-ledger-2026.md) supplies document images, provenance and the new email derivative.

## What the record supports

One complainant's related disputes produced a POST threshold closure and two different ODC lawyer-discipline outcomes. POST's August 13, 2025 correspondence states both a purview limit and a general five-year screen; its August 27 response expressly closes the matter. In May–June 2026 POST staff referred to public rules rather than allocating each disputed officer allegation to a ground, but did disclose a Case Status Committee review route on a non-anonymous request. The record does not establish whether that route was used or what the committee would have decided. See the submitted [POST complaint](../gitbook-export/mt-doj-public-safety-officer-standards-training-post-complaint-august-2025.md) and [correspondence reproductions](../gitbook-export/post-mortem-of-mt-doj-post-complaint-dismissal-august-2025.md).

ODC's Tipp file 25-147 received a substantive November 2025 determination with adverse reasoning. Later July 2026 letters did not map identified supplemental exhibits to that reasoning. The Supreme Court's October 2026 order affirmed on its stated untimeliness and request-framing grounds, not an allegation-by-allegation merits review. ODC's separate Lowney file 26-145 received a June 2026 limitations dismissal. The Commission's October 23 affirmance stated an evidentiary conclusion without clarifying whether it adopted the time bar or supplied an independent ground. Bucy's November 5 response **answered** Nuno's November 4 narrowed request by asserting that earlier correspondence explained ODC's reasoning, declining further information and pointing to Court review; it did not identify the requested passage. Neither an admission of no investigation nor a new Tipp disposition can be inferred. See the [case records and source ledger](../gitbook-export/montana-bar-complaints/odc-review-evidence-ledger-2026.md) and [detailed reasons analysis](../gitbook-export/research-reports-legal-advocacy-and-analysis/odc-time-bar-strategy-duty-to-give-reasons.md).

## What cuts against the original thesis

The original article conflated POST's two threshold grounds, misidentified Lowney's dismissal as a scope-of-representation decision, ignored substantive Tipp reasoning and later Commission/Court dispositions, and treated agency brevity as proof of deliberate suppression or unread evidence. Its claim that Police Commission members are unpaid and lack subpoena power conflicts with [Missoula Code ch. 2.30](https://ecode360.com/51617417) and [MCA § 7-32-4159](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0070/chapter_0320/part_0410/section_0590/0070-0320-0410-0590.html). The [city's policy](https://www.ci.missoula.mt.us/DocumentCenter/View/21236) describes findings, notice and a route to a Commission hearing; no case-specific Nuno MPD/Commission disposition was verified. A [2014 DOJ findings letter](https://www.justice.gov/sites/default/files/crt/legacy/2014/02/19/missoula_ltr_2-14-14.pdf) and [MOU](https://www.justice.gov/sites/default/files/crt/legacy/2014/06/10/missoula_settle_6-10-14.pdf) concern a historical County Attorney sexual-assault inquiry, not Nuno's later matter; the YWCA is not an MOU party. The historical MPD agreement was distinct.

## Narrowed inference and unresolved facts

The updated title is retained as publication history, not a legal finding of willful blindness or proof of intentional complaint suppression. The supported concern is that category-level closure explanations and subsequent non-clarifying responses can leave a complainant to infer which factual or legal premise to contest. There is no representative sample of Montana grievances, proven multi-agency coordination, documented case-specific MPD/Commission disposition, demonstrated unread exhibit, or proof that a POST committee request occurred. The Lowney review submission's published June 14 date and Commission's reference to a July 14 appeal remain unreconciled; no later Lowney Supreme Court filing was verified. The available record does not establish the complete agency files, a separate ODC explanatory document, or a controlling interpretation of how specific the reasons must be. The 2021 court-adopted rules, including their October 2021 amendment, were used rather than applying generally later-effective 2026 amendments retroactively: https://juddocumentservice.mt.gov/getDocByCTrackId?DocId=368130 .
