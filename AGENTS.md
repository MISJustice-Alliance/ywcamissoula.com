# Repository agent instructions

## Scope and authority

This is an independent civil-rights advocacy and accountability publication by MISJustice Alliance, not affiliated with YWCA of Missoula. Make the strongest evidence-supported case for people harmed by alleged misconduct, abuse of power, civil-rights violations, and malicious prosecution; advocacy does not authorize predetermined findings. These instructions apply across the repository and supplement source preservation, privacy, legal accuracy, non-affiliation, and authorization requirements. Check applicable nested instructions; surface conflicts affecting evidence, privacy, authorization, or editorial safeguards instead of silently weakening a requirement.

**Project-local skill library:** For a repository task, read [.agents/skills/README.md](.agents/skills/README.md), then load only relevant `SKILL.md` workflows. Read each required policy explicitly; links are not auto-loading. Skills confer no standing write, outreach, filing, publication, or deployment authority.

**Required editorial policy:** Before drafting, rewriting, summarizing, translating, titling, or reviewing public-facing editorial content, read [Editorial Style and Accountability Policy](EDITORIAL_STYLE.md). Apply it to article bodies, headlines, summaries, captions, metadata, structured data, and derivative content. If the policy cannot be accessed, report the blocker and do not mark the affected content publication-ready. This is a required task-specific instruction, not optional reading or an expansion of permissions; the policy supplements the baseline below. The link does not itself load the file.

For actual Notion-to-GitBook migration work only, read [Historical Notion-to-GitBook Migration Guide](NOTION_GITBOOK_MIGRATION_GUIDE.md), including the former CLAUDE.md appendix. [Claude Code routing](CLAUDE.md) also points to these instructions. The guide's historical roles, MCP examples, transformation procedures, rollback, and commit/push examples do not authorize present-day publication, deletion, or repository writes. Do not assume described models, integrations, or validators are deployed today; read the long historical guide in sections if truncated. For public-site changes, consult [Deployment and ownership map](DEPLOYMENT.md) for the current publication tree and checks; do not treat a successful repository edit as proof of live publication. For affected MPD statistics, read [open data issues](DATA_ISSUES.md) before reuse. For case-specific claims, follow the relevant source and complaint indexes rather than relying on this instruction file as evidence.

## Minimum editorial contract

Do not flatten the account into ‘both sides disagree.’

Fairness is accuracy, material completeness, and evidence-weighted analysis—not equal credibility or a manufactured midpoint. Do not sanitize, euphemize, excuse, or obscure supported wrongdoing. Identify actors, actions, chronology, authority, notice, consequences, and barriers to redress where supported.

Distinguish records, attributed accounts, allegations, institutional positions, adjudicated findings, analysis, and inference. Cite precise sources and dates; do not imply unread material was inspected. A complaint does not prove its allegations. A denial does not, by itself, rebut underlying records. Address material adverse evidence and revise, narrow, or withdraw conclusions when warranted. Do not infer intent, liability, innocence, or malicious prosecution beyond the evidence. Verify applicable law before stating legal elements or available remedies.

Protect sensitive records and preserve originals. Do not disclose private identifiers or move unredacted material into public files. External writes, publication, outreach, filings, and access changes require applicable approval for the exact action; historical migration permissions grant no standing authority.

Before marking public-facing content publication-ready, check the underlying evidence and the detailed policy. Do not mark it ready with false equivalence, unsupported accusations, omitted material contrary evidence, or unsourced material assertions; do not let headlines or metadata overstate the body. Record specific defects and needed evidence or qualification, and distinguish independent review from a separate pass by the drafting agent. Do not claim review, automated enforcement, tests, or publication occurred without verification.
