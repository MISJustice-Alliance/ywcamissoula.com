# ODC File No. 26-145 Rejection — Brian C. Lowney (June 9, 2026)

* **Document:** ODC-26-145-Rejection.pdf
* **Local GitBook asset:** [ODC File No. 26-145 rejection letter](../.gitbook/assets/odc-26-145-rejection-brian-lowney-june-9-2026.pdf)
* **Date of letter:** June 9, 2026
* **ODC file number:** 26-145
* **Respondent:** Brian C. Lowney, Esq.
* **Review deadline stated by ODC:** July 9, 2026

## Executive snapshot

The Office of Disciplinary Counsel dismissed the grievance concerning Brian C. Lowney on a limitations ground only. The letter states that ODC received the ethics grievance, then concludes that the claims are barred because the "charges happened in 2019," more than six years before the complaint was filed, and because ODC found no alleged facts supporting tolling under MRLDE 11(C) or an exception under MRLDE 11(D).

The dismissal letter does not address the grievance's substantive allegations of prosecutorial misconduct, Brady/Giglio violations, investigator conflicts, protected-speech suppression, or delayed dismissal after the deferred prosecution period. It instead identifies the limitations issue as dispositive and advises that a written request for Commission on Practice review must be submitted on or before July 9, 2026.

## Material language from ODC's dismissal

ODC's stated reason for dismissal is narrow and should be quoted precisely in any review request:

> "The claims in your grievance are barred by the statute of limitations. More specifically, your charges happened in 2019 which occurred in excess of six years from the date of the filing of your complaint, MRLDE 11(A), and no facts supporting a claim of tolling of the time limit, MRLDE 11(C), or exception to the time limit, MRLDE 11(D), were alleged."

ODC also advised:

> "As a result, ODC dismisses your grievance subject to your right to request review by the Commission on Practice. Should you wish to exercise your right of review, you must submit a written request to ODC on or before July 9, 2026, thirty (30) days from the date of this letter."

## Review significance

The key issue for review is not whether some events in the criminal case occurred in 2019. The key issue is whether ODC applied MRLDE 11 correctly to the misconduct actually alleged. The grievance alleged a course of prosecutorial conduct spanning the charging decision, the use or non-disclosure of impeachment and exculpatory information, the deferred prosecution agreement, the post-DPA dismissal posture, and later-discovered evidence of institutional conflicts and favorable material.

Under that posture, the Commission review request should focus on four questions:

1. Whether ODC incorrectly treated the grievance as a single 2019 event rather than a continuing or later-discovered violation.
2. Whether the grievance and related record alleged facts requiring analysis under MRLDE 11(B), 11(C), and 11(D), rather than only 11(A).
3. Whether ODC's limitations conclusion prematurely avoided the substantive prosecutor-duty issues raised by the grievance.
4. Whether the Review Panel should disapprove or modify ODC's disposition and direct further investigation under MRLDE Rule 10(C)(3).
