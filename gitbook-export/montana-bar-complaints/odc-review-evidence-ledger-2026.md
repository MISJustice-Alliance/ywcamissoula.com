The earlier [September 23 Commission transmittal](odc-25-147-september-23-2026-supreme-court-transmittal.md) reports a January 7 Tipp Commission decision, whereas the Supreme Court order refers to a February 9 notice letter. Those may be different events; the underlying Commission decision and notice are needed to reconcile them. The earlier [Lowney ODC dismissal](odc-26-145-rejection-brian-lowney-june-9-2026.md) states a different, limitations-based reason from the later panel's stated evidentiary rationale. Neither disciplinary outcome establishes the truth or falsity of every allegation or decides a separate civil claim.
