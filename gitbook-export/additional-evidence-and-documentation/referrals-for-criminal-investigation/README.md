## Scope and use

These pages are intended to support document-based referral review. They do not make charging decisions, do not provide legal advice, and do not replace investigator access to primary records, certified court files, dispatch records, police reports, communications metadata, and witness interviews.

## Suggested investigator-first reading order

1. Read the executive narrative in the referral report.
2. Review the supporting-excerpt table and identify each primary record still requiring certification or subpoena.
3. Review the statute-by-statute element notes.
4. Confirm venue and jurisdiction before deciding which agency or prosecutor should receive the referral.
5. Treat weak or disputed elements as investigative leads, not established facts.
