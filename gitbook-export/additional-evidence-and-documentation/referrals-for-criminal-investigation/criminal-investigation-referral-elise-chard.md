## Executive narrative

The updated source packet alleges that E'Lise Chard used her position at YWCA Missoula and her relationships with local law enforcement or YWCA-associated actors to escalate a personal dispute into protection-order, criminal-investigation, and public-defamation consequences for Elvis Ryland Nuno. The core allegation begins with a June 3, 2018 complaint Mr. Nuno submitted to YWCA Missoula concerning alleged employee misconduct. Nine days later, on June 12, 2018, E'Lise Chard allegedly filed a sworn petition for a temporary order of protection in Missoula County, Montana.

The source packet alleges the petition contained statements about repeated threats, harassment, dangerousness, criminal history, and Washington proceedings that were materially inconsistent with E'Lise Chard's later sworn hearing testimony. It states that, when Judge Sam Warren asked whether Mr. Nuno had contacted her beyond three 2017 emails, E'Lise answered "No," and when asked about threats, she answered "No threats." The source further states that Mr. Nuno did not contest the protection order and said he had no desire for contact.

The source packet then alleges a post-protection-order criminal phase involving Missoula Police Detective Connie Brueckner, who allegedly served simultaneously as a Missoula police detective and YWCA Missoula board member. It alleges Detective Brueckner used reports from Officer Ethan Smith, who had allegedly been removed from related matters for an "obvious appearance of impropriety," to obtain a search warrant and seize Mr. Nuno's work electronics. It also alleges E'Lise Chard received and forwarded an August 14, 2018 character-reference letter from witness P.S. that contradicted the dangerousness narrative, but that the letter was not disclosed to the defense, prosecutors, or court.

The source packet separately alleges E'Lise Chard made a September 2018 false report of "phantom phone calls" during the protection-order hearing, which subpoenaed phone records allegedly disproved. It further alleges that, from 2020 through 2024, E'Lise Chard recruited or coordinated with Tyleen Root to publish statements accusing Mr. Nuno of being a "stalker and woman beater," causing business and nonprofit losses. Finally, it alleges coordination with Danielle Chard / Bemis in connection with an August 2018 "lost in jail" or false-extradition incident.

All statements above are allegations from the source packet. The report below separates supported investigative leads from elements requiring corroboration.

### 1. Sworn protection-order allegations and alleged perjury

#### Concise narrative

The source packet alleges that on June 12, 2018, E'Lise Chard filed a sworn protection-order petition alleging ongoing threats, harassment, dangerousness, extensive criminal drug history, multiple Washington convictions or charges, and repeated contact. It further alleges that, during the protection-order hearing, she testified under oath that Mr. Nuno had made no contact beyond three 2017 emails and had made no threats. The source treats the petition and testimony as irreconcilable.

#### Element analysis

**Well-supported for investigation:** The source identifies a date, court context, alleged sworn petition content, alleged hearing testimony, and the judge. The claimed inconsistency is suitable for document-first verification through the petition and transcript.

**Partially supported or disputed:** Knowledge of falsity and materiality require a fact-specific assessment. The source packet alleges materiality because the petition affected protection-order and later criminal processes, but investigators need the full court record and any exhibits.

**Weak or unsupported:** The source packet states that all criminal charges were dismissed with prejudice, but dismissal alone does not prove perjury. The source also includes statements about a Washington criminal-history record that must be certified before being used as proof of falsity.

Federal obstruction, witness-tampering, mail/wire fraud, and RICO theories require separate federal elements and should be treated as screening issues rather than presently well-supported charging theories.

Venue would likely center in Missoula County, Montana for the direct E'Lise conduct. Federal venue may lie in the District of Montana for acts, communications, searches, detention, or official proceedings occurring there, with potential coordination with the Western District of Washington only if the false-extradition or Danielle-related communications are corroborated.
