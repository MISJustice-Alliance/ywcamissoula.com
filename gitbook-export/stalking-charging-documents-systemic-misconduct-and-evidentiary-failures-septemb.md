# Missoula Charging Documents

### Reader note

This page is an index hub, not the full argument. It is meant to help a lawyer or a non-lawyer reader quickly find the key pieces: the tainted investigation, the worst alleged offenses, the damages story, the harm to friends and family, and the P.S. email.

### At a glance

* Lawyer version: this is a map of the claims, proof, and downstream harm.
* Plain-English version: a broken investigation led to a bad story about what happened, and this page points to the evidence that explains why.
* Use the linked pages below for details; this page is the front door.

## 1. What this page is about

The September 2018 stalking charging documents are used here as a hub for the wider record. The core issue is not just the charging paper itself. It is the chain of alleged misconduct, the alleged use of compromised sources, the claimed suppression of favorable information, and the harm that followed.

## 2. Worst alleged offenses and why they matter

### Compromised primary source: Detective Ethan Smith

* Smith had a close personal relationship with E'Lise Chard.
* Smith was later removed from the matter due to obvious impropriety.
* The concern is that the charging narrative relied on a tainted investigative base.

### Fabricated or unsubstantiated statements

* Alleged phone conversation: both parents deny it occurred.
* Alleged mental-health statements: both parents deny making them.
* The concern is that contested statements were used as if they were reliable facts.

### Protected speech and due process

* The record elsewhere says Judge Warren ruled the YWCA complaint was protected speech.
* That matters because the case was not just about conduct; it was also about constitutional boundaries.
