# About this site — publisher, independence, record types, and corrections

## Who publishes this site

This site is published by **MISJustice Alliance**. The site content and its revision history are public in the GitHub repository [MISJustice-Alliance/ywcamissoula.com](https://github.com/MISJustice-Alliance/ywcamissoula.com).

## Independence

This site is independent. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by YWCA of Missoula, YWCA USA, the City of Missoula, or Missoula County. The official YWCA of Missoula website is a different site.

## How to read the material on this site

Pages on this site contain different kinds of material. They do not have the same weight. Use the label of each item before you rely on it.

| Label | What it is | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Allegation** | A statement that a person or the publisher makes about what happened. | Not a finding by a court, agency, or disciplinary body. |
| **Analysis** | The publisher's interpretation, legal argument, or synthesis of records. | Not a record, and not legal advice. |
| **Primary record** | A document as created by its author: correspondence, police report, filing, declaration, exhibit. | A record shows what its author wrote or filed. It does not prove that its contents are true. |
| **Complaint or grievance** | A document that asks an agency, a bar regulator, or a court to act. | Not a decision. |
| **Agency response** | A letter or decision from an agency (for example ODC, POST, OPA, DOJ). | Its scope is limited to what the agency decided, and it can be closed without a finding on the merits. |
| **Court order or judgment** | A document that a court issued. | A dismissal or plea outcome decides only what the order states. |
| **Adjudicated finding** | A fact that a court or tribunal found after a contested process. | — |
| **Dataset** | A structured list of records, events, or counts, with its source. | Counts on this site can be reported, derived, or unconfirmed. Each dataset page says which. |

Unless a page cites a court order or an adjudicated finding, statements about misconduct on this site are **allegations or analysis**. Pages that discuss named people describe the publisher's view of the record. The people named have not been found liable for the conduct described, unless a page cites a decision that says so.

## Corrections and contact

If a page is wrong, incomplete, or out of date, email **[contact@misjusticealliance.org](mailto:contact@misjusticealliance.org)**. Include:

1. The page URL.
2. The statement that you think is wrong.
3. A source that shows the correct information, if you have one.

The publisher reviews each request against the records. If a correction is made, the change is visible in the repository history.
