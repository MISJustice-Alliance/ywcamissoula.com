# Seattle OPA Complaint - 2016OPA-1167 (2016)

## Office of Professional Accountability: 2016OPA-1167

Official complaint filed with Seattle Office of Professional Accountability regarding police misconduct in [Case 2016OPA-1167](https://cr-2025-002-complaint-10_misjusticealliance.arweave.net/).

The complaint documents police report fabrication, unlawful detention, excessive bail, and systemic oversight failures involving the transformation of a police report as it passed through three law enforcement agencies.

### OPA correspondence (primary records)

* [October 25, 2016 — OPA closure notice and Mr. Nuno's same-day reply](.gitbook/assets/seattle-opa-2016opa-1167-closure-correspondence-2016-10-25.pdf). OPA states the complaint was reviewed by the intake sergeant, civilian Director, and civilian Auditor; that SPD "were operating from the information provided by the subject of the complaint and properly forwarded the complaint"; that charging and bail decisions belong to the prosecutor and court; and that no further action will be taken.
* [December 2, 2016 — OPA declines to reopen, and Mr. Nuno's reply](.gitbook/assets/seattle-opa-2016opa-1167-no-reinvestigation-correspondence-2016-12-02.pdf). OPA states that the additional information was consistent with what it had already received, that "OPA does not re-investigate complaints," and that the matter is closed. Mr. Nuno's reply quotes Edmonds Officer Samuel Gagner's report #2016-00009660: "since Nuno did not attempt to contact Chard or have another party contact her on his behalf, there was no violation of the order."
