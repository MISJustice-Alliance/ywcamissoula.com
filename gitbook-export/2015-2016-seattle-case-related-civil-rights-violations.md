# 2015-2016 Seattle Case & Related Civil Rights Violations

This document analyzes civil rights violations occurring in Seattle during 2015-2016, including malicious prosecution, excessive bail, and Fourth Amendment violations.

## Key Issues

* Malicious prosecution claims
* Excessive bail practices
* Fourth Amendment search and seizure violations
* Due process concerns

## Timeline of Events

Documentation of events spanning 2015-2016 in Seattle jurisdiction related to the broader pattern of civil rights violations.

## Case Outcome: Dismissal with Prejudice

On **August 23, 2016**, Seattle Municipal Court Case No. 613225 (City of Seattle v. Elvis Nuno) was **dismissed with prejudice**. The stated reason for dismissal was **insufficient proof (lack of evidence)**, as reflected on the order signed by the judge and the prosecutor. See the [dismissal order (Seattle-Case-613225-Dismissal.png)](.gitbook/assets/seattle-case-613225-dismissal.png).

Source-packet records further state that a subpoena of Danielle Chard's phone and text records produced by the prosecution showed **bi-directional communication** during the period in which she claimed "cyber stalking and harassment" — evidence of voluntary contact and dialogue, with no evidence of threats of any kind. (Source packet: Declaration §§ VIII–IX; Prosecutor Cover Letter § IV, per the [Criminal Investigation Referral — Danielle Chard Bemis](additional-evidence-and-documentation/referrals-for-criminal-investigation/criminal-investigation-referral-danielle-chard-bemis.md).)
