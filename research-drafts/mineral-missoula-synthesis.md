## A. Executive answers to the five questions

1. **What happened?** A September 2022 mandamus campaign challenged Mineral County's disclosures of officer impeachment/qualification information; contemporaneous reporting quotes the petitions, describes Halligan's untimely-disclosure ruling, and reports at least nine dismissals, but the signed mandamus, Halligan, Marks, settlement, fee, and dismissal orders were not independently obtained. Marks reportedly found **no Brady violation in his particular case** while warning the prosecutor about future disclosures. Separately, the Montana Supreme Court held in August 2024 that Kunzelman, Lindauer, and Visintin lacked statutorily compliant preappointment evaluations by qualified providers during specified DUI investigations; it reversed three district-court orders that had reversed evidence suppression. It did not decide the other eight petitioners' merits. [Daily Montanan](https://dailymontanan.com/2022/10/09/lawsuit-accuses-mineral-county-officials-of-misconduct-asks-for-audit-of-court-cases/); *Patrick v. Mont. Fourth Jud. Dist. Ct.*, No. OP 24-0341, slip op. at 1–5, 8–10 (Mont. Aug. 13, 2024) (order), [official PDF](https://juddocumentservice.mt.gov/getDocByCTrackId?DocId=490058).
2. **Did DOJ investigate?** **Not established by the records located.** Jasper's call for AG/U.S. Attorney intervention was a request, not an opening notice; Montana DOJ's June 2024 response in OP 24-0341 was advocacy defending prosecutions, not an investigative report. No DCI opening/referral/closure number or Prosecution Services Bureau investigation record for the Mineral disclosure controversy was located in official indexed material or the inspected court records. Public-web absence cannot exclude nonpublic inquiries. POST certification issues and reported discrete POST proceedings for Visintin, Nobles, and Allard do not establish a DCI inquiry into Kunzelman or Mineral prosecutors. [Daily Montanan](https://dailymontanan.com/2022/10/09/lawsuit-accuses-mineral-county-officials-of-misconduct-asks-for-audit-of-court-cases/); State's Resp. to Pet. for Writ of Supervisory Control 11–19, No. OP 24-0341 (Mont. June 28, 2024), [official PDF](https://juddocumentservice.mt.gov/getDocByCTrackId?DocId=479757); [Mineral research](mineral-research.md#sources).
3. **Independent courts and relief?** The Supreme Court found the provider defect undisputed, held § 7-32-303(5), MCA (2023), forfeited the relevant peace-officer powers, rejected a separate prejudice requirement and the State's de facto-officer/private-person theories on these facts, granted supervisory control to Patrick, Ovitt, Green and reversed Vannatta's three suppression reversals. Justice-court dismissals of eight other petitioners were **without prejudice** and State appeals were pending in August 2024; their supervisory petitions were denied for jurisdiction/process, not merits. No appellate order here vacated convictions or finally dismissed three charges. *Patrick*, slip op. at 1–2, 8–11. [Official docket](https://juddocumentservice.mt.gov/getCaseInformation?caseId=OP%2024-0341) records administrative closure Sept. 27, 2024. Later district proceedings need their own dockets.
4. **Missoula/Nuno link?** A reported 2013 Missoula **County Sheriff's Office** termination/email is potential impeachment-record provenance for Kunzelman's Mineral prosecution; Jordan Kilby's earlier **County Attorney's Office** employment is personnel overlap while she represented defendants against Mineral. Missoula **city police** and Nuno actors are not shown to have participated in those Mineral proceedings. Missoula-based judges are judicial actors, not institutional prosecution links. The Nuno corpus concerns distinct Missoula MPD/prosecution/YWCA allegations and documents; no common Mineral actor, instruction, policy, or Nuno evidentiary chain is demonstrated in the inspected records. [Daily Montanan](https://dailymontanan.com/2022/10/09/lawsuit-accuses-mineral-county-officials-of-misconduct-asks-for-audit-of-court-cases/); [corpus manifest](corpus-research.md).
5. **Meaningful law and failure of analogy?** *Brady*, *Giglio*, § 46-15-322(1)(e),(4) and *Severson* guide trial disclosure and identification of a specific undisclosed, favorable item, government custody/knowledge, materiality, and remedy; *Ruiz* restricts a federal impeachment-before-plea claim; *Howard* imposes preservation and a substantial-need showing for generalized personnel-file requests; *Parker* rejects a freestanding civil Brady claim where no judicial proceeding was affected. The officer-appointment forfeiture in *Patrick* is not applicable to unrelated MPD conduct without the statutory qualification defect. [Law memorandum](law-research.md), with opinion links and pinpoints.
